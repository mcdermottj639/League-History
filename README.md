# 🏈 Nectars Bolonga — League History

Thirteen seasons of the league (2013–2025), plus the weekly power rankings.

**→ https://mcdermottj639.github.io/League-History/**

Open it, tap your name, and the whole thing starts talking to you: your
seasons, your medals, your playoff record, your Cum Bowls, your row highlighted
wherever it appears. Nothing is sent anywhere and there is no account — the
name you pick is stored on your own phone.

### What's in it

- **🏆 Rankings** — the commissioner's set for the week, with a take on every team
- **Honours** — champions, the trophy case, and who's still waiting
- **Seasons** — every final table, 2013 to 2025
- **Records** — the record book, the luck index, rivalries, seeds and upsets
- **Cum Bowl** — every play-in game, and who the league's worst really was
- **You** — your thirteen seasons

Every finish is mapped to a person and cross-checked against ESPN's own owner
column. Where a number comes from playoff games only, it says so.

Static site — HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript. No backend, no tracking.
